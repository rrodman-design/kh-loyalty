The Arc

From Pricing Program
to Loyalty Ecosystem

Heinz Verified didn't start as a loyalty program. It started as a pricing program — and evolved into something far more powerful.

"We wanted to evolve beyond transactional relationships — because pricing alone only drives short-term savings. It's not going to create long-term loyalty from operators."

— Elizabeth Riggle, Head of Loyalty, KH Away From Home
1
The Problem
Independent Operators Left Behind

Small restaurants couldn't access the pricing power of big chains. No volume, no leverage — they paid more for the same products.

2
2024 — Foundation
Heinz Verified Pricing Launches

A dedicated pricing program gave independents up to 25% off through their distributor. It worked — but it was only transactional.

3
May 2026 — Live
Loyalty Layer Launches on Salesforce

Built on Salesforce Loyalty Cloud — points, tiers, a digital portal, and a rewards store. 1,500 operators in 90 days.

4
2026–2027 — Next
AI, Agents & A Community

Agentforce loyalty agent, personalized bonus point AI, mobile app, and an operator community — Heinz Verified becomes the most valuable relationship any food brand has with small restaurants.
