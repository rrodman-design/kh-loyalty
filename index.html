How We Got Here

From a Pricing Problem to a Loyalty Platform

This didn't happen overnight. Heinz Verified started as a simple question: why can't independent operators get the same deals as big chains? The answer became a program that changed the relationship.

"We wanted to evolve beyond transactional relationships — because pricing alone only drives short-term savings. It doesn't create long-term loyalty."

— Elizabeth Riggle, Head of Loyalty, KH Away From Home
1
The Problem
Independents Were Getting Left Behind

Small restaurants couldn't access the pricing power of big chains. No volume, no leverage — they paid more for the same Kraft Heinz products.

2
2024 — First Move
Heinz Verified Pricing Launches

Up to 25% off for independent operators through their distributor. It worked — operators loved it. But it was transactional. No relationship. No data. No future.

3
May 2026 — The Leap
Loyalty Launches on Salesforce

Built on Salesforce Loyalty Cloud — points, tiers, a digital portal, a rewards store, and automated point tracking from distributor data. 1,500 operators in 90 days.

2026–2027 — What's Next

📈 The Automation Win

In July 2026, purchase-based points went fully automated — flowing directly from distributor billback data. Every case an operator already buys now earns points automatically. No friction. No manual uploads. That's the unlock that makes this scalable.

👥 The Growth Driver Coming Next

The first 1,500 enrollments came from just 18 field reps, email campaigns, and organic social. Broker teams — who cover the majority of the US — are now being onboarded to promote Heinz Verified. That's when enrollment really accelerates.

🏆 What Operators Are Redeeming

Tabletop Heinz caddies, neon signs, branded to-go boxes, patio umbrellas, free samples. For an independent operator on a tight margin, free branded supplies have real dollar value — and brand affinity that money can't directly buy.

📸 The Platform Advantage

Because Heinz Verified runs on Salesforce, every enrollment flows directly into Sales Cloud. Reps and brokers see who joined, what tier they're on, and when to follow up. Marketing, sales, and loyalty are all working from the same data for the first time.

The Target: 5,000 by End of 2026

With broker teams coming online and the points automation live, the path to 5,000 enrolled operators by end of year is clear. The foundation is built. The acceleration phase is just starting.
