The Arc of Heinz Verified

From Discount to Devotion

Heinz Verified didn’t start as a loyalty program. It started as a pricing program — and evolved into something far more powerful.

1
The Problem

Independent Operators Left Behind

Small restaurants couldn’t access the pricing power of big chains. No volume, no leverage. They were paying more for the products they needed most.

2
2024 — Foundation

Heinz Verified Pricing Launches

A dedicated pricing program gave independent operators up to 25% off — direct from Kraft Heinz, through their distributor. It worked. But it was only transactional.

3
May 2026 — Live

Loyalty Layer Launches on Salesforce

Built on Salesforce Loyalty Cloud, the program adds points, tiers, a digital portal, and a rewards store. Operators now earn for every purchase they were already making.

4
2026–2027 — Next

AI, Agents & A Community

Personalized promotions, an Agentforce loyalty agent, a mobile app, and an operator community — transforming Heinz Verified into the most valuable relationship any food brand has with small restaurants.

“

We wanted to evolve beyond transactional relationships and build a loyalty feature — because pricing alone is only going to drive short-term savings. It’s not going to create that long-term value or loyalty from operators.

— Elizabeth Riggle, Head of Loyalty, Kraft Heinz Away From Home

Where Loyalty Is Today

Three Tiers. One Platform. Real Value.

Heinz Verified is live on Salesforce Loyalty Cloud. Operators log in, track their points, unlock rewards, and move through tiers based on what they were already buying. The program runs on top of the existing pricing program — amplifying value without replacing it.

Built on Salesforce, the loyalty portal is connected to the Commerce site, giving the team a unified view of every operator relationship — marketing, sales, and service all in one place.

The big unlock: Purchase-based points now flow automatically from distributor billback data. Every case purchased = points earned. No manual uploads. No friction.
